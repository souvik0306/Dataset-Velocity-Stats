# 10 September AI Super High vs RAW High-Dynamic Motion Metrics

AI and RAW flights are evaluated over 10 s, 20 s, and 30 s windows.
Each configured start is Vicon cutoff + 5 s; each end is start + window duration.
Velocity and acceleration use the cleaning and metric definitions in `rosbag_motion_metrics.py`.
